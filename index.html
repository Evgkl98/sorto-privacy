Privacy Policy

Sorto for iOS · Last updated 31 August 2026

Data collection

Sorto does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No account is required to use the app, and no information is sent to the developer or to any server.

Camera and photos

The camera is used solely to capture an image of a waste item for classification. Images are processed entirely on your device using an on-device machine learning model. They are never uploaded, saved, or shared, and are discarded immediately after processing.

Third parties

Sorto contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking software. No data is shared with third parties.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the app's behaviour changes. The date above reflects the most recent revision.
